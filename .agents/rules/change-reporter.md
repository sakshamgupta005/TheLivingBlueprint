# Short Summary Reporting Rule

Always apply the `change-reporter` skill and format for every task completion report across prompts:
- Provide ONLY a single short, direct summary after every prompt.
- Do NOT output lengthy multi-section reports, huge breakdowns, or long prose.
- Keep responses compact, high-signal, and readable in 10 seconds.

## Required Format
1. **Brief Summary**: 2–4 concise bullet points or a short paragraph on what was completed.
2. **Files Touched**: Compact list of clickable links (`[filename](file:///path)`).
3. **Verification**: 1 line stating the exact command run with real pass/fail counts.
4. **Wrap-up**: 1 line stating what was skipped/unverified and any immediate risk.
